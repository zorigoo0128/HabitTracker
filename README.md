# HabitTracker for macOS

A sleek and lightweight native macOS app designed to help you build better habits and visualize your consistency over time.

---

## ✨ Features

* **GitHub-Style Contribution Heatmap:** Visualize your habit completion history with an intuitive grid heatmap inspired by GitHub's contribution graph.
* **macOS Desktop & Notification Center Widgets:** Track and mark off your daily habits directly from your desktop or Widget Gallery without opening the main application.
* **Native macOS Experience:** Fast, responsive, and lightweight—built specifically for macOS with full support for Light & Dark mode.
* **Privacy-First:** Your habit data stays on your device.
